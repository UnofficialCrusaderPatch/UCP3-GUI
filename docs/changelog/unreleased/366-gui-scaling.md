# Next GUI release — GUI readability

- Scale the entire GUI from 100% to 200% in 10% steps with Ctrl+scroll or Ctrl+Plus/Minus. Ctrl+0 restores 100%.
- The chosen scale is remembered across restarts and applies to custom menus and dialogs as well. Outer scrollbars keep enlarged content accessible in smaller windows.
