# Next GUI release — disabling AI packs

- Turning off an AI pack now warns about affected AI selections and removes references to that pack from your customizations. The next selected AI takes over, or plugin/game defaults are restored. Reopening the AI Swapper menu is no longer necessary to correct the saved configuration.
- Cancelling the warning keeps your selections and configuration unchanged.
