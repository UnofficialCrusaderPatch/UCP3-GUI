# GUI 1.0.16

Unreleased.

- Compact tables for troop choices and resource amounts in updated modules. Related options can collapse with their description. ([#368](https://github.com/UnofficialCrusaderPatch/UCP3-GUI/pull/368))
- Creator mode: mark settings or whole groups as required or suggested. Hover over a changed setting's marker to reset it. ([#369](https://github.com/UnofficialCrusaderPatch/UCP3-GUI/pull/369))
- Enlarge the GUI with Ctrl+scroll or Ctrl+Plus/Minus, from 100% to 200%. Ctrl+0 resets the size; your choice is remembered. ([#366](https://github.com/UnofficialCrusaderPatch/UCP3-GUI/pull/366))
- Resize the window from its top edge and upper corners again. ([#367](https://github.com/UnofficialCrusaderPatch/UCP3-GUI/pull/367))
- Removing an AI selection now also removes it from the game configuration. ([#362](https://github.com/UnofficialCrusaderPatch/UCP3-GUI/pull/362))
- Show the reset bin only for settings you have explicitly changed.
- Disabling an AI pack warns about affected selections and updates them immediately. Cancelling keeps your choices. ([#365](https://github.com/UnofficialCrusaderPatch/UCP3-GUI/pull/365))

The updated AIV Troop Behaviour and Starting Troops/Resources modules are separate downloads. Their new tables require GUI 1.0.16.
